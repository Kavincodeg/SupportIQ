# SpotifyCares Customer Support Intent Taxonomy

## Overview & Methodology
This taxonomy was derived empirically from actual customer support conversations between Twitter users and **@SpotifyCares**, sampled from the final deliverable corpus [`data/spotify_grounding_corpus_v2.csv`](file:///c:/Users/Kavin/Downloads/Hiver/data/spotify_grounding_corpus_v2.csv) (26,085 pairs).

## Honest Clustering Caveat & Methodological Discussion

> [!NOTE]
> **Methodological Disclosure (Relevant to "What is Misleading About My Headline Number" Report Section):**
> Sentence embeddings were generated using `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (384 dimensions), and K-Means clustering was tested across $k = 5$ to $12$. Across all tested $k$ values, the silhouette scores were uniformly low ($0.027$ to $0.051$):
>
> | $k$ Value | Silhouette Score | Inertia |
> | :---: | :---: | :---: |
> | $k=5$ | 0.0509 | 134.72 |
> | $k=6$ | 0.0353 | 131.69 |
> | $k=7$ | 0.0471 | 129.57 |
> | $k=8$ | 0.0347 | 127.86 |
> | $k=9$ | 0.0358 | 126.48 |
> | $k=10$ | 0.0291 | 125.15 |
> | $k=11$ | 0.0270 | 123.41 |
> | $k=12$ | 0.0371 | 122.67 |
>
> These low silhouette scores indicate weak natural clustering structure in short, noisy tweet text that contains idiosyncratic handles, emojis, truncated links, and varied phrasing.
>
> **The final taxonomy was primarily derived through manual reading and qualitative categorization of the 250 sample messages by a human reviewer**, using k-means clustering only as a rough discovery aid rather than a definitive grouping. This distinction is treated honestly: **the taxonomy is not purely "unsupervised" or "data-driven" in a strict algorithmic sense**, but rather a qualitative domain schema guided by full-sample empirical inspection and informed by clustering centroids.

## Final Intent Distribution (N=250 Sample)

Following a systematic re-scan of the 250 samples, actionable free-tier advertising experience complaints (7 items), upgrade inquiries (1 item), and family invite issues (1 item) were folded into **Subscription & Billing Issues**, while miscellaneous actionable messages were correctly mapped to their respective domain categories.

| Intent Category | Count | Coverage (%) | Primary Support / Escalation Domain |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1. Subscription & Billing Issues** | 59 | 23.6% | Billing Ops, Tier Monetization, Ad-Experience Complaints |
| **2. Playback & Technical Errors** | 45 | 18.0% | Client Engineering, OS bug logging, cache troubleshooting |
| **3. Feature Requests & Device Support** | 42 | 16.8% | Product Management, OS/Hardware Roadmap teams |
| **4. Content Availability & Licensing** | 41 | 16.4% | Music Licensing, Regional Rights, Artist Ops (SfA) |
| **5. Playlist & Library Management** | 24 | 9.6% | Community support, UI guidance, curation feedback |
| **6. Account Access & Security** | 20 | 8.0% | Security Ops, Trust & Safety, immediate account freeze |
| **7. Other / Unclear (Catch-All)** | **19** | **7.6%** | Frontline triage, bare links, uninterpretable fragments |
| **Total** | **250** | **100.0%** | |

> [!TIP]
> The catch-all category (**Other / Unclear**) now stands at **7.6%** (down from 13.6%), containing strictly uninterpretable fragments, bare links, or greetings. Substantive complaints are no longer dumped into the catch-all bucket.

## Critical Boundary Distinctions (Preventing False Merges)

1. **Free-Tier Ad Complaints vs. Catch-All:**
   - *Example:* *"Please stop playing creepy Halloween movie ads while I'm in the gym by myself at 1am"* (`sample_id=145`), *"Stop upsetting my baby with gunshot sounds every other song"* (`sample_id=43`), *"Turn down the volume on your damn commercials? -3db > 0db #noisewars and don't give me that crap about premium!"* (`sample_id=164`).
   - *Resolution:* Folded into **Subscription & Billing Issues** (Option A). Advertising content, frequency, and loudness are the core operational differentiators between the free tier and a paid premium subscription.
2. **Subscription Cancellation vs. Account Takeover / Hacked:**
   - *Example:* *"I need to cancel my account but my email was hacked and I don't have access to it"* (`sample_id=230`).
   - *Distinction:* If an account is actively compromised or the user cannot log in because an attacker changed their email, it must be handled as **Account Access & Security** first. A standard billing cancellation link will fail if the user cannot log in.
3. **Language & Localization Display Sync vs. Account Security:**
   - *Example:* *"How can I change the language on Spotify? Somehow it’s Spanish on my phone and English on other devices"* (`sample_id=100`).
   - *Distinction:* Display synchronization and localization settings bugs belong strictly to **Playback & Technical Errors**. Account Access & Security is reserved strictly for genuine security compromises (hacks, lockouts, credential theft) to prevent dilution of high-priority escalation triggers.
4. **Catalog / Regional Licensing vs. Playback Bug:**
   - *Example:* *"When is #reputation coming to Spotify?"* (`sample_id=171`) vs. *"App freezes whenever this song comes up in the queue"* (`sample_id=9`).
   - *Distinction:* Missing songs, unreleased albums, or greyed-out tracks due to territorial copyright belong strictly in **Content Availability & Licensing**. They do not represent app bugs.
5. **10,000 Song Library Cap vs. General Feature Request:**
   - *Example:* *"Remove your frustrating 10k song library limit"* (`sample_id=135`).
   - *Distinction:* Classified under **Playlist & Library Management** because it directly restricts active library addition and playlist saving behaviors.

## Detailed Taxonomy Rubrics & Examples

### 1. Subscription & Billing Issues
- **Definition:** Customer requests regarding payments, unexpected or recurring charges, premium plan tiers (Family, Student, Duo), promotional pricing, subscription cancellation, refunds, or free-tier advertising experience complaints.
- **Coverage:** 23.6% (59 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"@SpotifyCares Hi for some strange reason i was charged 14.99 for premium and headspace this month, i havent changed anything and all previous months it has been 4.99 as i am a student. I have changed it back to student now but dont know why it had changed?"* (`sample_id=6`)
  2. *"@SpotifyCares how do i cancel 30 day free trial"* (`sample_id=91`)
  3. *"@115888 please stop playing creepy Halloween movie ads while I’m in the gym by myself at 1am thanks  @SpotifyCares Thank you! 😌"* (`sample_id=145`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Subscription & Billing Issues' whenever the user expresses concern regarding payment processing, billing discrepancies, subscription tiers (such as Student verification via SheerID, Premium Family plan invites/pricing/address verification, or free trial expirations), cancellation inquiries, refund requests, or comparative questions about the benefits/perks of upgrading from Free to Premium. This category also explicitly includes substantive complaints regarding the free-tier advertising experience—such as ad frequency, inappropriate or disturbing ad content (e.g. horror movie promos, gunshot sound effects), or jarring ad volume/loudness jumps—because ads are the defining monetization mechanism and differentiator between the free tier and a paid subscription. If a user complains about paying for Premium while asking why a specific song or album is missing from the catalog, prioritize 'Content Availability & Licensing' instead.

---

### 2. Playback & Technical Errors
- **Definition:** Customer reports of application crashes, system freezes, songs failing to load or play, audio glitches, language/localization display bugs, corrupted offline downloads, or installation errors across platforms.
- **Coverage:** 18.0% (45 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"Hey @115888 , I'm getting real tired of not being able to listen to music and have the app crash all the time.  Please fix it. https://t.co/c1kQ9g7Hej  @SpotifyCares It's Spotify version 8.4.22.857 armV7 on a Samsung Galaxy S8+ running Android 7.0  @SpotifyCares Nope. Logged out of Spotify, restated phone, listened to one song on the playlist and it still froze up..."* (`sample_id=9`)
  2. *"@SpotifyCares windows Error 17 on trying to install on PC tried all troubleshoot options , not happy , £9.99 a month and it doesn’t work ! Help please  @SpotifyCares It’s windows 7..."* (`sample_id=13`)
  3. *"@SpotifyCares I have lost all offline tracks (AGAIN) but have 6GB of spotify files stuck on SD Card that won't delete after uninstall. Help  @SpotifyCares Ok thanks, I will give it a go."* (`sample_id=8`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Playback & Technical Errors' when the customer reports acute runtime bugs, application crashes, freezes, installation failures (e.g., Error 17, Error 50), blank screens, device display synchronization or language/localization settings bugs (e.g., app displaying in the wrong language across devices), offline downloads getting corrupted or deleted, or songs failing to play due to technical failure (e.g., infinite spinner, web player playback stopping, or audio stuttering). Do not use this intent if a song cannot be played specifically because it is greyed out, removed by copyright/licensing, or not yet released in the catalog; those belong to 'Content Availability & Licensing'.

### 3. Feature Requests & Device Support
- **Definition:** Customer requests for new software capabilities, UI improvements, hardware/OS platform integrations (e.g., Apple Watch, Apple TV, iPhone X, CarPlay), or algorithmic suggestions.
- **Coverage:** 16.8% (42 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"@115888 please tell me you’re going to release an AppleWatch app soon!? Want to run w/o phone!"* (`sample_id=48`)
  2. *"When is @115888 going to put a sleep timer in their settings?  Because I could use that right now."* (`sample_id=183`)
  3. *"@SpotifyCares  is there a way to block explicit songs from random playlists. I can’t listen to these at work."* (`sample_id=179`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Feature Requests & Device Support' when the customer proposes product enhancements, UI redesigns, missing software tools (such as sleep timers, explicit lyric filters, or lyrics integration), new hardware/platform support (e.g., Apple Watch standalone streaming, CarPlay responsiveness, smart speaker integration, Apple TV, or iPhone X display optimization), or international rollout requests (e.g. launching in India or Russia). This category covers forward-looking requests rather than complaints about existing features breaking.

---

### 4. Content Availability & Licensing
- **Definition:** Inquiries or complaints regarding missing songs, unreleased or delayed albums, regional licensing restrictions, greyed-out tracks, or incorrect artist metadata.
- **Coverage:** 16.4% (41 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"@115888 where is 'Reputation'?! https://t.co/fc04Wpbakl  @SpotifyCares I already have heard them.. I want to listen to the album  @SpotifyCares I hope so"* (`sample_id=106`)
  2. *"It sucks that #LEMONADE is still not on @115888...guys, c'mon! I need more @12899 in my life."* (`sample_id=26`)
  3. *"Blegh, some of the old Get Scraped Deamau5 tracks got removed from spotify ;(  @SpotifyCares https://t.co/G6J9keroaj #10 'bored of canada' is greyed out, doesn't show up in searches  @SpotifyCares Should be Netherlands"* (`sample_id=37`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Content Availability & Licensing' when a user asks about music or podcast content that is not found, was removed from Spotify, is greyed out in their country, has not yet been released on the platform (e.g., asking when an anticipated album like Taylor Swift's Reputation will arrive), or inquires about regional chart availability (e.g., Top Charts for a specific country). This also includes artist metadata errors (e.g., songs mapped to the wrong artist profile, incorrect bios, or artist release mismatches). If a track is available in the catalog but fails to stream due to app crashes or network bugs, assign to 'Playback & Technical Errors'.

---

### 5. Playlist & Library Management
- **Definition:** Questions, issues, or feedback regarding playlist creation, editing track order, changing cover art, managing saved collections, home screen playlist preferences, or hitting the 10,000-song library limit.
- **Coverage:** 9.6% (24 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"Dear @115888 - it would be super handy if we could edit the order of songs in playlists on phones... https://t.co/ZOLl1uzl0H  @SpotifyCares THANK YOU!!  👏🏻💃🏼👏🏻"* (`sample_id=44`)
  2. *"@SpotifyCares why can't i change  the cover/description of my playlists?? it seems like everyone else can but me?? 😔  @SpotifyCares yes!  @SpotifyCares 1.0.20.101"* (`sample_id=96`)
  3. *":'-(  @SpotifyCares let me know when you remove your frustrating 10k song library limit.  https://t.co/vANgjQflxL"* (`sample_id=135`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Playlist & Library Management' when the user's inquiry focuses directly on organizing, curating, or configuring their playlists and music library. This includes reordering tracks, sorting alphabetically, uploading playlist artwork or descriptions, recovering deleted playlists, managing home screen playlist displays, navigating Daily Mixes / Discover Weekly curation, and venting or inquiring about the platform's 10,000-song saved library cap ('Epic collection my friend'). If the issue is that downloaded songs were wiped from local storage by a technical glitch, classify as 'Playback & Technical Errors'.

---

### 6. Account Access & Security
- **Definition:** Urgent customer issues regarding compromised/hacked accounts, password resets, unauthorized profile modifications, third-party social account unlinking, forgotten login credentials, or multi-device login conflicts.
- **Coverage:** 8.0% (20 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"@115888 someone is hacking my account; Why can't I readily find a support phone # or email to fix it? It's shitty and you know it."* (`sample_id=15`)
  2. *"@SpotifyCares my account has been hijacked and they also changed my email. I just reset my password tho and I hope it won’t happen again. I’m a premium user so please take this as a serious matter now that I know some ppl are complaining the same."* (`sample_id=245`)
  3. *"@SpotifyCares Uh spotify, why did you just log me out then tell me my email doesn't exist when I try to reset my password?  @SpotifyCares Your support team is slower than your programming monkeys - it was fixed by the time you responded."* (`sample_id=177`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Account Access & Security' when a user is locked out of their Spotify account, suspects unauthorized access, reports that an attacker changed their email or password, is trapped in password reset loops, requests unlinking of connected third-party social accounts (e.g. disconnecting a relative's Facebook profile), or notices concurrent playback from unknown remote devices ('account used elsewhere'). This category takes high priority because compromised accounts require immediate security escalation and identity verification workflows.

---

### 7. Other / Unclear (Catch-All)
- **Definition:** Bounded catch-all category for truly ambiguous tweets, standalone links or screenshots with no context, brief acknowledgments/gratitude, or unrelated inquiries.
- **Coverage:** 7.6% (19 / 250)
- **Verbatim Examples from Data:**
  1. *"@SpotifyCares Thx spot"* (`sample_id=47`)
  2. *"@SpotifyCares https://t.co/777rIXhISF"* (`sample_id=71`)
  3. *"@181186 i want to work with you guys. Is it possible? I have a degree in computer information"* (`sample_id=207`)
- **Classification Rubric (System Prompt for Classifier):**
  > Classify under 'Other / Unclear' only as a last resort when the message cannot be definitively mapped to any of the specific support intents. Common examples include brief conversational acknowledgments ('thx spot', 'props for the creativity'), bare image/link URLs with no explanatory text ('https://t.co/777rIXhISF', 'why https://t.co/...'), vague statements of distress with no actionable details ('@SpotifyCares please help'), or off-topic inquiries such as job applications.
